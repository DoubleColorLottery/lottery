## Informational Notes

### I-01: Zero factory commission

The factory does not charge the Flap reference commission. This is intentional: 100% of distributable token tax is assigned to the lottery vault and then to the prize pot. There is no developer withdrawal route in the vault.

### I-02: Guardian-controlled beacon

Flap Guardian can replace the forwarding-vault implementation until it calls `lockVaultUpgrades`. Guardian cannot upgrade the immutable lottery, change its eligibility signer, or withdraw the lottery pot through this beacon. Locking is permanent.

## Centralization Analysis

The vault has no operator or owner functions. `sync` and `flush` are permissionless. The factory is the beacon owner only so its Guardian-gated methods can administer the beacon. No non-Guardian account can upgrade or lock it.

The wider lottery system trusts a separate EIP-712 eligibility signer. A compromised signer could issue an incorrect account certificate, although onchain aggregate holder and ticket totals cannot exceed the prepared manifest. The manifest is an audit commitment, not an onchain balance proof. This is an explicit product tradeoff and the signer must be isolated and monitored.

## Static Analysis Triage

Slither was run across the Flap lottery, vault, and factory. Its reports were reviewed as follows:

- The VRF funding and request calls are inside `nonReentrant` owner-only functions and target the immutable coordinator selected at deployment. Accounting after `deposit` is intentional: a failed deposit keeps the BNB and adds it to the pot, while a successful deposit transfers it to the subscription.
- The vault's reported arbitrary ETH send targets the immutable lottery decoded during one-time proxy initialization. `flush` clears accounting before the guarded call, and a failure reverts the entire state change.
- The remaining unused-return reports are OpenZeppelin delegate-call helpers or the unused diagnostic value returned alongside `ECDSA.tryRecover`; neither changes the success checks.
- The timestamp reports on enum comparisons are flattened-source mapping false positives. Round cadence and timeouts use block numbers; timestamps are recorded only as metadata.
